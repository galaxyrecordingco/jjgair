About JJ Gair

Music has been part of JJ’s life for as long as he can remember. Growing up surrounded by the sounds of country, folk, and rock, he picked up the guitar at a young age and never put it down. Over the years, he’s developed a sound that blends heartfelt originals with tasteful covers that audiences know and love.

JJ Gair Music – Solo Performances

When it’s just JJ and his guitar, the music takes on an intimate, personal tone. His solo sets showcase the storytelling at the heart of his songwriting, whether it’s an original tune or a well-loved classic delivered with fresh feeling

JJ Gair and Da Country Outlaws

Most often, JJ is joined on stage by Da Country Outlaws — a group of seasoned musicians who share his love for traditional country and roots music. Together, they bring high-energy performances that get crowds singing, dancing, and feeling part of the show.

From small-town bars to outdoor festivals, JJ and the Outlaws have built a reputation for delivering music with heart, soul, and a little grit.

Meet Da Country Outlaws

  • JJ Gair – Guitar & Vocals

  • Lori Smith– Guitar & Vocals

  • James Williams – Bass Guitar & Vocals

  • Ron Poorman Jr – Drums & Vocals

Each member brings years of experience, their own musical influences, and a passion for live performance. Together, they create a sound that’s both deeply rooted in tradition and fresh for today’s audiences.

Glen Barrows — Guest Guitarist


Glen is a frequent live performer with JJ Gair and Da Country Outlaws, adding his signature guitar sound to the band’s mix of originals and covers. A lifelong musician with deep country roots, he brings both energy and heart to the stage whenever he joins the lineup.
